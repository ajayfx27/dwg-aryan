Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how the DWG ALGO landing page handles information. Please review it before using the website.

Information collected

This website does not provide a registration or payment form. It uses the Meta Pixel, which may collect website visit and interaction data, including technical/browser information, subject to Meta's technologies and policies. A custom event is sent when the Telegram button is clicked. A button click does not confirm a Telegram membership.

Purpose

We use the Meta Pixel to understand website visits and measure advertising interactions. Meta may process information under its own privacy policies.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on Vercel. Clicking the join button takes you to Telegram, which operates under its own privacy policy. Meta Pixel is provided by Meta Platforms. These services may process data independently.

Your choices

You can control some tracking through browser settings and Meta's advertising preferences. Browser controls may not block all tracking.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, please use the contact information on our Contact page. A direct email contact must be added by the site owner before publication.

This statement should be reviewed against the actual hosting, tracking configuration, and applicable legal requirements before publishing.
